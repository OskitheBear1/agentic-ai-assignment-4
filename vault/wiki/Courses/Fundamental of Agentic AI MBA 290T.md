# Fundamental of Agentic AI MBA 290T

This course explores the fundamentals of building and evaluating AI agents using programming, software systems, and machine learning techniques. Students will gain practical skills in designing, implementing, and deploying AI agents, with a focus on conceptual fluency and responsible AI practices.

## Logistics
- Lectures: Tuesdays 4:00 – 6:00 pm PT in N570

- Last class: Tuesday, October 6, 2026

- Final assignment (Assignment 5) due: Tuesday, October 13, 2026

## Grading
- Attendance is required and is worth 20% of your grade.
- Assignments 1–4 (lowest score dropped) are 50% of the final grade.
