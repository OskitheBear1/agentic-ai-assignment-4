# Negotiaions MBA 252 Fall 2026

This note outlines the syllabus for Negotiaions MBA 252 at UC Berkeley for Fall 2026. It covers the course description, expectations, grading breakdown, policies on attendance, participation, and late assignments, the weekly schedule, and details about assignments and recommended readings.

## Course Description
- Negotiaion is a frequent occurrence in business and life, involving power, trust, and cognitive biases.
- The course aims to build negotiation comfort and confidence, teach analytical frameworks, and explore the psychology of negotiation.
- It combines theory of value creation and claiming with practical skill development through weekly negotiation exercises.

## Course Expectations & Ground Rules
- The course is experiential and requires active engagement, as participation impacts peers' learning.
- Role instructions are confidential and should not be shared before or during negotiations.
- Experimentation with tactics is encouraged, but physical intimidation or harassment is not permitted.

## Grading
- Global participation (attendance, engagement, discussion quality): 40 points
- Preparation (worksheets submitted before class): 20 points
- Feedback (to negotiation partners): 20 points
- In-class quizzes: 20 points (2-3 quizzes)
- Press team assignment: Up to 3 bonus points

## Policies
- Notify the professor via email at least 24 hours in advance for missed classes.
- Missed class with 24+ hour notice: -2 points; without notice: -3 points.
- First missed class is free with advance notice.
- Missing more than 30 minutes of class: -1 point (late arrival/early departure); -2 points without notice.
- Global participation is assessed based on engagement and attentiveness.
- Students are expected to attend their registered section.
- Electronic devices are prohibited during lecture unless explicitly required for an exercise.
- Late assignments are penalized -1 point per day late; preparation worksheets are never accepted late.
- Cheating includes reading confidential role information, consulting others/the internet, and plagiarism; results in 0 points and a report to the Office of Student Conduct.
- Anonymized negotiation outcomes may be used for research with voluntary participation; data deletion requests are welcome.

## Weekly Schedule
- Week 1 (8/26): Fundamentals: Yerba Mate Thompson Ch. 1, 3
- Week 2 (9/2): Integrative Negotiaion I: New Bike Thompson Ch. 2, 4
- Week 3 (9/9): Salary Negotiaions: Salary Negotiation Thompson p.370-378; job-offer readings (bCourses)
- Week 4 (9/11): Integrative Negotiaion II: The Grand Strannd Getting Past No p.31-104; bCourses readings
- Week 5 (9/23): Power in Negotiaions: Myti-Pet Food Thompson p.102-124, 240-250; Getting Past No p.105-158
- Week 6 (9/30): Real negotiation analysis, group processes, how to say no — Prep for Challenger discussion
- Week 7 (10/7): Negotiation IRL (guest speaker) + special fun activity
- Week 8 (10/14): Hostage negotiators!! Trust: Bullard Houses Thompson Ch. 6, p.351-360; deception readings (bCourses)
- Week 9 (10/21): Shadow negotiations, social influence & coalitions: Fastskin Ch. 7, 12; persuasion readings (bCourses)
- Week 10 (10/28): Cross-functional team negotiaion: Best Stuff on Earth Cross-Functional Team Negotiations (bCourses)
